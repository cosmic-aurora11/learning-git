## 📍 Current Situation
The party has returned to <span class="recap-location">Phandalin</span> after carving through <span class="recap-location">Tresendar Manor</span> and confronting <span class="recap-npc">Glasstaff</span> himself. With Glasstaff dead and any chance of extracting information from him gone, the group's best lead now lies with the <span class="recap-npc">Drow agent</span> of the <span class="recap-faction">Black Spider</span>, who has offered a dangerous trade: the <span class="recap-item">puzzle box</span> in exchange for <span class="recap-npc">Gundren</span>.

---

## ⚡ Events
- The heroes battled through the Manor's remaining <span class="recap-faction">Redbrand</span> defenders, cutting down the last organized resistance beneath <span class="recap-location">Tresendar Manor</span>. In the aftermath, they discovered <span class="recap-npc">Sildar</span>, captured during his search for <span class="recap-npc">Iarno</span>.
- Within <span class="recap-npc">Glasstaff's</span> workshop, the group chose to dismantle his operation completely, wrecking much of the laboratory after scavenging what valuables and tools they could, including a <span class="recap-item">Potion of Invisibility</span>.
- <span class="recap-npc">Glasstaff</span> confronted the party with the <span class="recap-npc">Sentinel</span> at his side, using the construct as both shield and weapon while the battle sprawled across the room. Even as the fight turned against him, Glasstaff remained composed, convinced he still controlled the board.
- When defeat became inevitable, he attempted to bargain. In exchange for ending the attack, <span class="recap-npc">Glasstaff</span> offered information regarding <span class="recap-npc">Gundren</span> and the <span class="recap-faction">Black Spider</span>. The offer was refused. Realizing he had lost leverage, Glasstaff burned the documents in his possession and fled the Manor.
- Outside, the party emerged to find Pompo, <span class="recap-npc">Sildar</span>, and armed townsfolk closing in on <span class="recap-npc">Glasstaff's</span> escape route. As the crowd argued over what should be done with him, <span class="recap-npc">Bart</span> forced his way forward, knife in hand. Rock attempted to stop him, insisting Glasstaff still held answers they needed, but grief and anger drowned out reason. Bart killed Glasstaff where he stood, ending the reign of the <span class="recap-faction">Redbrands</span> in <span class="recap-location">Phandalin</span>.
- The group later returned to town to find <span class="recap-npc">Bart</span> shaken by what he had done. In gratitude for freeing <span class="recap-location">Phandalin</span> from the <span class="recap-faction">Redbrands</span>, he offered the party lodging at the inn.
- Awaiting them there was a message from the <span class="recap-npc">Drow agent</span>: a proposal. The <span class="recap-item">puzzle box</span> for <span class="recap-npc">Gundren</span>. The exchange was to take place at the abandoned <span class="recap-location">Old Crossroads Saloon</span>, east of Phandalin, the following day.
- Finally given a moment to breathe, the heroes turned their attention once more to <span class="recap-npc">Gundren's</span> <span class="recap-item">puzzle box</span>. Through persistence and careful focus, they unlocked it.
- A crimson glow spilled outward, staining the room's shadows red. Inside rested two <span class="recap-item">rubies</span>, one glowing steadily, the other pulsing faintly like a heartbeat. As the box opened, the air tightened around them. A metallic taste filled their mouths. Pressure settled behind their eyes. For a brief instant, the glow traced the silhouette of a blade across the stone before fading back into silence.
- Using his forgery kit, Pompo crafted replica <span class="recap-item">rubies</span>, while the genuine stones were split between Macha and Rock.
- Later that night, long after the others had gone to sleep, a quiet knock came at Eris' door. Waiting outside stood <span class="recap-npc">Sister Garaele</span>, cloaked and alone.

---

## 🔍 Learned
- <span class="recap-npc">Sildar's</span> investigation revealed that <span class="recap-npc">Iarno Albrek</span>, originally sent to <span class="recap-location">Phandalin</span> by the <span class="recap-faction">Lord's Alliance</span>, abandoned his mission, seized power within the town, and became the figure known as <span class="recap-npc">Glasstaff</span>.
- Rock uncovered a recipe for <span class="recap-item">Mushroom Broth</span>, capable of granting temporary vitality.
- Within <span class="recap-npc">Glasstaff's</span> study, the party uncovered records concerning <span class="recap-location">Wave Echo Cave</span> and the legendary <span class="recap-location">Forge of Spells</span>. Centuries ago, dwarves and gnomes forged an alliance to mine the cave's vast mineral wealth and harness its magical energy through the Forge, where enchanted weapons and items could be created. The mine was ultimately destroyed during a devastating assault by orcs, and its location was lost beneath collapse and ruin.
- Additional correspondence revealed that <span class="recap-npc">Glasstaff</span> had been recruited by the <span class="recap-faction">Black Spider</span> to abduct <span class="recap-npc">Gundren</span> and deliver him to the goblin <span class="recap-npc">Yeemik</span> in exchange for weapons forged through the power of <span class="recap-location">Wave Echo Cave</span>. Later messages confirmed that Gundren had already been transferred and that the Black Spider intended to interrogate him personally.
- The notes also made one thing unmistakably clear: the <span class="recap-faction">Black Spider</span> knows about the party and wants them dead.
- A final note from the <span class="recap-npc">Drow</span> awaited the heroes at <span class="recap-location">Bart's Inn</span>: "*Meet me at <span class="recap-location">Old Crossroads Saloon</span> at Noon. I will bring him. You bring the <span class="recap-item">puzzle box</span>.*"

## 🧩 Unresolved
- The party defeated <span class="recap-npc">Glasstaff</span> and were unable to stop his murder before learning everything he knew about the <span class="recap-faction">Black Spider's</span> plans and <span class="recap-npc">Gundren's</span> condition.
- The <span class="recap-npc">Drow's</span> offer may be genuine, a trap, or something far more complicated.
- The purpose of the <span class="recap-item">Unique Key</span> remains unknown, though <span class="recap-npc">Sister Garaele</span> and <span class="recap-npc">Bart</span> believe it was crafted to open something ancient and significant.
- <span class="recap-npc">Sister Garaele</span> has privately summoned Eris, likely regarding matters tied to the <span class="recap-faction">Order of the Gauntlet</span>.
- Within <span class="recap-location">Tresendar Manor</span>, the party uncovered more about each other because of the <span class="recap-npc">Nothic</span>: Macha cannot read; Pompo’s bloodline is cursed three generations deep; and Rock stole his cauldron from his town, and it holds magic he does not yet understand. Now that the fighting has settled, what else might these four strangers be hiding from each other?

---

## 🎯 Stakes
- The <span class="recap-faction">Black Spider's</span> plans have advanced unchecked, and <span class="recap-npc">Gundren</span> is now fully in his grasp. If the Black Spider succeeds in extracting the location of <span class="recap-location">Wave Echo Cave</span>, Gundren's value, and his life, may quickly run out. The meeting at the <span class="recap-location">Old Crossroads Saloon</span> may be the party's final opportunity to reach him before the trail disappears entirely.
