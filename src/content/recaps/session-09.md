## 📍 Current Situation
The party now stands face to face with the <span class="recap-npc">Drow agent</span> of the <span class="recap-faction">Black Spider</span> inside the <span class="recap-location">Old Crossroads Saloon</span>. Before them sits <span class="recap-npc">Gundren</span>, battered and unconscious. The offer remains unchanged: the <span class="recap-item">rubies</span> in exchange for Gundren.

---

## ⚡ Events
- Late in the night, <span class="recap-npc">Sister Garaele</span> brought Eris to her secluded cottage. After probing questions about Eris' values and character, Garaele revealed the truth beneath the home: the <span class="recap-faction">Order of the Gauntlet</span> served as a front for the secretive organization known as the <span class="recap-faction">Harpers</span>. In the cellar, Eris met Harper agents of many races and backgrounds and was formally inducted into their ranks. She received both the <span class="recap-item">Ring of Falcon Force</span> and a piece of enchanted <span class="recap-item">amber</span> capable of calling for Harper aid.
- Back at the <span class="recap-location">inn</span>, Eris shared news of her induction with the group. As preparations began for the meeting with the <span class="recap-npc">Drow</span>, <span class="recap-npc">Bart</span> privately spoke with Rock. Though the two remained divided over <span class="recap-npc">Glasstaff's</span> execution, Bart stood firm in his belief that he had acted for the good of <span class="recap-location">Phandalin</span>.
- <span class="recap-npc">Bart</span> also delivered a locked box recovered from <span class="recap-npc">Glasstaff's</span> rented room. After Macha picked the lock, the party uncovered a magical weapon attachment known as the <span class="recap-item">Orb of Misty Step</span>.
- Before departing town, the group visited <span class="recap-npc">Percival Brightspark</span>, purchasing the <span class="recap-item">Coin of Fortunate Misfortune</span> and a <span class="recap-item">Sympathetic Wick</span>. At <span class="recap-npc">Gil's</span> forge, Macha attached the <span class="recap-item">Orb of Misty Step</span> to his weapon, while Rock reinforced the striking power of his greataxe.
- Leaving <span class="recap-npc">Bart</span> and <span class="recap-npc">Gil</span> positioned roughly a mile behind as backup, the party traveled toward the <span class="recap-location">Old Crossroads Saloon</span>.
- Along the road, the heroes encountered a band of <span class="recap-npc">goblins</span>. With no shared language to bridge the gap, suspicion quickly gave way to violence. After watching several companions fall, the surviving goblins fled into the wilderness shouting the only phrase the party could understand: "<span class="recap-npc">Yeemik</span> talk!"
- Arriving at the <span class="recap-location">Old Crossroads Saloon</span>, the party slipped inside through a locked side entrance and began clearing the building room by room. In both the basement and first floor, they discovered carefully arranged piles of bones positioned as though awaiting reanimation. Choosing caution over risk, the group scattered and disabled the remains before they could rise.
- Hidden within the saloon's piano, the heroes uncovered a <span class="recap-item">Flame Crossbow</span>.
- On the second floor, the party finally came face to face with the <span class="recap-npc">Drow agent</span> of the <span class="recap-faction">Black Spider</span> and <span class="recap-npc">Gundren</span> himself, bound to a chair and visibly beaten. The Drow calmly repeated her offer: the <span class="recap-item">rubies</span> in exchange for the dwarf.
- When Pompo attempted to conceal the existence of forged <span class="recap-item">rubies</span>, the <span class="recap-npc">Drow</span> immediately saw through the deception. Now, with <span class="recap-npc">Gundren</span> finally within reach, the party must decide how far they are willing to trust the enemy standing before them.

---

## 🔍 Learned
- <span class="recap-npc">Sister Garaele</span> revealed that the <span class="recap-faction">Harpers</span> operate secretly within the region, and Eris has now been formally inducted into their ranks.
- <span class="recap-npc">Glasstaff</span> possessed hidden magical equipment and upgrades, including the <span class="recap-item">Orb of Misty Step</span>.
- The <span class="recap-location">Old Crossroads Saloon</span> had been prepared defensively, with skeletal remains deliberately positioned for possible necromantic reanimation.
- The <span class="recap-npc">Drow</span> immediately recognized Pompo's forged <span class="recap-item">rubies</span>, confirming she knows far more about the <span class="recap-item">puzzle box</span> and its contents than the party previously realized.

---

## 🧩 Unresolved
- The party has reached <span class="recap-npc">Gundren</span>, but he remains unconscious and in the <span class="recap-npc">Drow's</span> control.
- The heroes must decide whether to surrender the real <span class="recap-item">rubies</span>, attempt another deception, fight, or find some other way to break the stalemate.
- The connection between the rubies, the <span class="recap-item">puzzle box</span>, and the <span class="recap-location">Wave Echo Cave</span> remains unclear.
- The meaning behind the <span class="recap-npc">goblins'</span> warning, "<span class="recap-npc">Yeemik</span> talk," remains uncertain.
- If the <span class="recap-npc">Drow</span> already knew the <span class="recap-item">rubies</span> had been forged, the extent of the <span class="recap-faction">Black Spider's</span> knowledge about the party may be far deeper than expected.
- The <span class="recap-faction">Black Spider's</span> larger plan is still moving, and the party does not yet know how much time Gundren has left.

---

## 🎯 Stakes
- The heroes have finally reached <span class="recap-npc">Gundren</span>, but the <span class="recap-npc">Drow</span> now holds the advantage. If negotiations collapse, the party risks losing both Gundren and the <span class="recap-item">rubies</span>, along with any chance they have of uncovering the secrets of <span class="recap-location">Wave Echo Cave</span>.
