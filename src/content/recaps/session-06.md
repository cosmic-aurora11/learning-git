## 📍 Current Situation
The party has pushed deeper beneath <span class="recap-location">Tresendar Manor</span>, now certain that <span class="recap-npc">Gundren</span> is no longer here. What remains is unfinished business: <span class="recap-npc">Glasstaff</span>, the growing instability within the hideout, and whispers of a creature being called into this world, a <span class="recap-npc">Sentinel</span>.

---

## ⚡ Events
- While searching the weapons locker, Pompo uncovered a concealed passage opening into a cavern that seemed older than the manor itself, a hollow carved by something that had lived there long before the <span class="recap-faction">Redbrands</span> claimed it.
- A <span class="recap-faction">Redbrand</span> guard, drawn by the items left on the entrance steps, came to check in on the party. Though the party attempted to mask the evidence of battle, the ruse failed. The guard fled shouting for reinforcements. He never reached his allies, but his warning echoed through the tunnels.
- Macha vaulted into the fray from outside, and the party emerged victorious, though no longer unnoticed. After the chaos, Rock's <span class="recap-item">cauldron</span> and Eris' <span class="recap-item">stone</span> were reclaimed.
- Recognizing the rising danger, Eris volunteered to escort <span class="recap-npc">Mirna</span> and her children back to town, also delivering the recovered weapons to <span class="recap-location">Phandalin</span> so the town could arm itself. <span class="recap-npc">Gil</span> chose to remain, determined to see <span class="recap-npc">Glasstaff</span> answer for his crimes.
- Deeper within the cavern, the party encountered the resident horror, a <span class="recap-npc">Nothic</span>, its single eye gleaming with hunger and knowledge. It offered secrets in exchange for flesh, truth, and a peculiar demand: the <span class="recap-item">eyepatch</span> of a creature in the adjacent chamber. The fallen <span class="recap-faction">Redbrands</span> became tribute. Rock, ever the chef, seasoned the offering.
- To secure the eyepatch, the party confronted three <span class="recap-npc">Bugbears</span>, agents of the <span class="recap-faction">Black Spider</span>, sent to oversee <span class="recap-npc">Glasstaff's</span> work. Their defeat freed a grateful goblin named <span class="recap-npc">Fartbucket</span>, who fled into the night.
- Pressing onward, the heroes burst into a chamber of intoxicated <span class="recap-faction">Redbrands</span>. Surprise carried them through the opening moments, and four fell before blades were fully drawn, but a brawl erupted in the confined room and echoed through the manor's depths.

---

## 🔍 Learned
- The <span class="recap-npc">Nothic</span> revealed that <span class="recap-npc">Glasstaff</span> is in the eastern chambers, engaged in the summoning of a <span class="recap-npc">Sentinel</span>, a construct resistant to slashing steel but vulnerable to ice. It also disclosed the location of a hidden cache of scrolls and weapons.
- <span class="recap-npc">Gundren</span> was taken from the manor less than two days ago by the <span class="recap-faction">Black Spider</span>.
- The <span class="recap-npc">Bugbears</span> confirmed the power dynamic: <span class="recap-npc">Glasstaff</span> answers to the <span class="recap-faction">Black Spider</span>, just as the <span class="recap-npc">Necromancer</span> once did. The <span class="recap-faction">Black Spider's</span> reach extends beyond this town and beyond this manor.
- In payment for knowledge, the party offered secrets of their own: Macha cannot read; Pompo's bloodline is cursed three generations deep; and Rock stole his <span class="recap-item">cauldron</span> from his town, and it holds magic he does not yet understand.

---

## 🧩 Unresolved
- <span class="recap-npc">Glasstaff</span> is still alive, and the <span class="recap-npc">Sentinel</span> summoning is underway.
- The <span class="recap-faction">Black Spider</span> now has <span class="recap-npc">Gundren</span>, and their destination remains unknown.

---

## 🎯 Stakes
- If the party hesitates, the summoning will grow stronger, deepening the <span class="recap-npc">Sentinel's</span> power and tightening <span class="recap-npc">Glasstaff's</span> hold over it, and with each passing hour, the <span class="recap-faction">Black Spider</span> widens the distance between them and <span class="recap-npc">Gundren</span>, pulling him further into whatever design is unfolding.
