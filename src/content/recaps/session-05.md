## 📍 Current Situation
The party has infiltrated the <span class="recap-faction">Redbrand Hideout</span> beneath <span class="recap-location">Tresendar Manor</span> and freed the prisoners. <span class="recap-npc">Gundren</span> was not among them, but witnesses place him in the hideout within the last two days.

---

## ⚡ Events
- Using <span class="recap-npc">Bart's</span> help to confirm <span class="recap-faction">Redbrand</span> membership codes, Pompo infiltrated the popular Redbrand bar, the <span class="recap-location">Sleeping Giant</span>. Pompo, posing as Cornelius Strongbottom, convinced <span class="recap-npc">Officer Roland</span> of his "Auditor" credentials and gained the location of the hideout.
- The group found the dilapidated <span class="recap-location">Tresendar Manor</span> and its secret entrance via the cellar. Using their personal items, they narrowly avoided setting off a trap at the entrance.
- Pompo's Wild Magic Surge limited his ability to lie, but he convinced the guards that Eris was a prisoner. Rock slipped by using the <span class="recap-item">Scroll of Invisibility</span>.
- The party bypassed another trap and reached the family crypt, guarded by two skeleton soldiers. Pompo initially passed unscathed due to his disguise, but Eris hastily tried to repel the skeletons, leading to a skirmish.
- At the prison, Pompo failed to convince the guards of his <span class="recap-faction">Redbrand</span> membership, leading to another brawl. A note on one of the defeated Redbrands provided a clue to opening the sealed weapons locker in the next room.
- With their victory secured, the party freed the prisoners and found <span class="recap-npc">Mirna</span>, her two children, <span class="recap-npc">Gil</span>, and <span class="recap-npc">Sheriff Clint</span>. Enraged by the Redbrands, Gil requested the weapons in the armory to arm himself and the town, to assist the heroes.
- With the safe combination clue and their collective noggins, the party cracked the code and opened the weapons locker.

---

## 🔍 Learned
- The <span class="recap-faction">Redbrands</span> use verbal call-and-response codes to identify each other ("*Is the cellar still dry?*" "*Dry enough to hide a dagger*" and "*What's the thread color tonight?*" "*Scarlet. Always.*").
- In addition to the location of the <span class="recap-faction">Redbrand Hideout</span>, <span class="recap-npc">Officer Roland</span> in the Sleeping Giant shared that prisoner movement was coordinated by <span class="recap-npc">Officer Desmere</span> at the hideout.
- The prisoner <span class="recap-npc">Mirna</span> saw a stocky dwarf believed to be <span class="recap-npc">Gundren</span> being transferred less than two days ago.
- Bart's friends have been found. <span class="recap-npc">Gil</span> the blacksmith was found alive in the prison. <span class="recap-npc">Sheriff Clint</span> was found dead.

---
